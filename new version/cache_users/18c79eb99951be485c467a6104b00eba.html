Scrooged

1988

Directed by Richard Donner

Synopsis

The spirits will move you in odd and hysterical ways.

Frank Cross is a wildly successful television executive whose cold ambition and curmudgeonly nature has driven away the love of his life. But after firing a staff member on Christmas Eve, Frank is visited by a series of ghosts who give him a chance to re-evaluate his actions and right the wrongs of his past.

Review by Rafael "Mister Movie" Jovine 20

After hearing about it for most of my life, I finally got to see it, and boy did I like it. I enjoy stories that take a classic and give it a funny twist while retaining the essence of the overarching storyline.

I can't see a child seeing the dead and ghosts in this film and not being traumatized. They not only did an excellent job with the make-up, but they are also rather eerie and frightening. Richard Donner does a good job of preserving the quirky essence of 80s and 90s kids movies while also making it accessible to adults. Elfman's music captures both the lighthearted and the very gloomy components of the picture, which seemed to come effortlessly…
