Review by Chris Evangelista

Springtime for Hitler, but real.

In 1997, Princess Diana died as the result of a violent car accident. Now, thanks to DIANA: THE MUSICAL, Diana’s tragic death is the *second* worst thing to happen to her. 

I watched this while stoned and I was still shocked at how the entire thing feels like a very long parody of a real musical. It’s like Evita rewritten as fan fiction. 

Amazing stuff, in all the wrong ways. “It’s the thrilla in Manila with Diana and Camilla.” — actual lyric.
