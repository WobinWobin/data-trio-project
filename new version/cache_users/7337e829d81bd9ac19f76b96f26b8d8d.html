There Will Be Blood

2007

Directed by Paul Thomas Anderson

Synopsis

There will be greed. There will be vengeance.

Ruthless silver miner, turned oil prospector, Daniel Plainview, moves to oil-rich California. Using his son to project a trustworthy, family-man image, Plainview cons local landowners into selling him their valuable properties for a pittance. However, local preacher Eli Sunday suspects Plainview's motives and intentions, starting a slow-burning feud that threatens both their lives.

Review by Northernlion 63

This review may contain spoilers. I can handle the truth.

I keep thinking of the scene midway through the film where Daniel and Henry make it to the Pacific Ocean and go for a swim. Having finally secured the land to build his pipeline and faced with the realization there is nothing left to conquer, he tries to be a normal man for one night but it doesn't suit him. He's as out of place at the Peachtree dance as he is cutting a figure on the Pacific coast; a strange frontier man looking quite alien in the water when he seems so at home in the dirt and mud.

Here we realize Daniel Plainview cannot be like other men. Other men drink for thirst, or for mirth. But Daniel is an oil man: he drinks until the well is dry and then he drinks yours too, because that is what oil men do.
