Birdemic: Shock and Terror

2010

Directed by James Nguyen

Synopsis

Why did the eagles and vultures attack?

A platoon of eagles and vultures attacks the residents of a small town. Many people die. It's not known what caused the flying menace to attack. Two people manage to fight back, but will they survive Birdemic?

Review by Ira Brooker 2

If you follow me on this site, you know I watch a lot of bad movies. Like, bad movies. I do it out of a genuine love for the form, which makes me a little irked when something like Sharknado comes along and the internet bands together to snicker at a self-aware "bad" movie that only aspires to make internet people snicker at it. I feel the way I imagine hardcore drinkers do when they refer to Saint Patrick's Day as "Amateur Night."

I put off watching Birdemic for a long time because I'd heard so much hype that I thought it was going to fall into that category. I was wrong. This is a legitimate bad movie. A terrible, wonderful, lunatic piece of filmmaking that deserves its reputation among the greats of the genre. This is a thing of beauty.
