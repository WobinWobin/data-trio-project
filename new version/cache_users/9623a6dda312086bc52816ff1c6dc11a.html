Review by Allison M. 🌱

The Week Of starts out alright, with the chemistry of Adam Sandler and Chris Rock helping the movie take off. Now, they interact with each other less and less directly as the movie goes on, so that is part of the problem.

Chris Rock plays the most unconvincing doctor (he takes phone calls in the middle of surgery, even with blood on his hands). Some of the situations or scenes were funny. It's not so bad. Just not that great, either. Kind of in the same vein as Click: some good moments, but a lot of stupid stuff that bring it way down.

Vegan alerts:
-The options for the wedding guests are chicken and fish.
-A whole roasted chicken is shown.

Vegetarian points:
A character is vegetarian.
