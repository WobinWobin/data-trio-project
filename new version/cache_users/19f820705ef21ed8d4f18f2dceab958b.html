Review by Lexzilla 8

Pretty similar in story to the first one, only without any of the attempted thoughtfulness or tension that lurked underneath, but with bloodier violence and bigger shootouts. It’s not better, but it is trashier. So, you know, a fair trade off.

The other big change up in this one, is that instead of going after general bad guys and miscreants, this time Bronson is hot on the trail of the exact men that raped his daughter (that poor girl did not have a good time in either film, bless her). And we are finally gifted the catharsis of revenge.

I’ve got to admit, I kind of like the fact that this has given up any pretence of being a film about something. It’s just there for people to enjoy watching Bronson shoot folks. That’s the kind of sleazy fun I can get behind.
