Review by Ian West 2

Didn’t think I’d dig this but I was wrong. Felt like some of the Scream tv show and Riverdale by way of some weirdo tv Goosebumps movie and I’m cool with that. Even though I’m kinda over fake 80’s aesthetic and synth overload at this point it doesn’t really turn me into a nostalgia sux ‘get off my lawn’ movie nerd like it does for many, plus I liked the cast, atmosphere, soundtrack, and pizza centric plot since I used to work in a pizza place and “deliver the ‘za”

Unless I’m in the right headspace A24 stuff usually sinks any good mood I’m in and makes me reflectively depressed? But this Goofy horror comedy ghost/witch/werewolf whodunit murder jam with some not so subtle messages about political manipulation by groups/media manipulation via politicians was exactly what I was looking for last night. 

Plus I ate pizza while I watched it so... it was just the right amount of cheese for a good night.

A24 Ranked

I’ll be honest: Based on the delays, the terrible trailer, and the fact that it was literally dumped onto VOD quietly by its distributor, I wasn’t expecting Slice to be good, but I did not expect it to be one of the absolute worst films I’ve seen in 2018 (and of all time).

Slice is less a horror-comedy, more a colossal failure of an attempt at an SNL skit stretched out to an overstuffed, tedious 82 minutes of abysmal filmmaking & writing and terrible acting all around.
