Hatchet II

2010

Directed by Adam Green

Synopsis

Victor Crowley Lives.

Picking up right where the original ended, Marybeth escapes the clutches of the swamp-dwelling killer Victor Crowley. After learning the truth about her family’s connection to the hatchet-wielding madman, Marybeth returns to the Louisiana swamps along with an army of hunters to recover the bodies of her family and exact the bloodiest revenge against the bayou butcher.

Review by Ian West 5

Hatchet II continues the blood soaked swamp excursion, in many ways outdoing the gore fest of the first film, opening strong and giving us some more Crowley lore and numerous appearances by genre veterans before settling into a lengthy buildup before the first non intro/non flashback kill happens... then the bloodbath gates open big time. Honestly I’m not the biggest fan of the Mary Beth casting change... but I’ll survive. The comedy still works for me (for the most part) and the kills are gleefully drenched in splatter, which is just about what you’d want from a sequel such as this.

I was lucky enough to see this theatrically before the issues with the MPAA put a stop to it and I gotta say, Hatchet films are fun to see with a big group of people.
