The Promise

Directed by Terry George

Synopsis

Empires fall. Love survives.

Set during the last days of the Ottoman Empire, a love triangle develops between Mikael, a brilliant medical student, the beautiful and sophisticated artist Ana, and Chris, a renowned American journalist based in Paris.

Review by Christian Ha

I share the same concern I had with Titanic, though it doesn't do it as much, but this movie sometimes seems to prioritize the romance over the horrific backdrop of atrocities. I fail to understand why filmmakers insist on intertwining romance with such tragic events. However, The Promise faced significant criticism upon its initial release, particularly from individuals who denied the occurrence of these horrific events. Nevertheless, I appreciate that this film sheds light on this devastating genocide, as it has been largely overlooked in comparison to other tragic events of the same period. The Promise, despite its noble ambitions and epic scope, failed to engage me. It lacked emotional connection with the characters, suffered from a lack of focus, and had poor pacing. I believe the Armenian people deserve a better film that exclusively highlights the atrocities and heroes of that horrible period in history
