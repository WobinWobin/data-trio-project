Review by Tasha Robinson 4

I was so taken with A Girl Walks Home Alone At Night, but this follow-up from the same writer-director feels like a distracted misfire. It's packed with stuff — a community of muscle-porn desert cannibals, Jim Carrey getting one really funny scene in a wholly silent role, a female protagonist who's down two limbs from the baseline number, and Keanu Reeves as a cult leader. What it doesn't have is a theme or a goal or an ending that justifies any of this. It's kind of a slow, sleepy Mad Max with some really lousy fortune-cookie philosophy at the end.

Full TIFF reports on the way at TheVerge.com.
