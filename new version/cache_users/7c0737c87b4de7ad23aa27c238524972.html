Southland Tales

2006

Directed by Richard Kelly

Synopsis

Have a nice apocalypse.

Set in the futuristic landscape of Los Angeles on July 4, 2008, as it stands on the brink of social, economic and environmental disaster. Boxer Santaros is an action star who's stricken with amnesia. His life intertwines with Krysta Now, an adult film star developing her own reality television project, and Ronald Taverner, a Hermosa Beach police officer who holds the key to a vast conspiracy.

Review by Will Sloan

This movie is a big box of loaded and evocative sounds and images, from which you are invited to assemble your own meaning. It gains a few more fans every year for how its very Bush-era concerns about the intersection of capitalism, right-wing politics, and the surveillance state with celebrity and influencer culture stays relevant. There are ideas and aesthetics here that would be at home in a 2006-era "Bushisms" website, and stuff about live-streaming, faux-progressive tech barons, and ineffectual left-wing activist groups that feels like it was written today, and 2006 and 2023 quickly bleed into each other. The aesthetic is relentlessly ugly and tacky throughout, and while virtually all of the characters are fools, Kelly doesn’t distance himself…
