Review by Georgia Coley 13

I absolutely despise God's Not Dead. And to be clear, it has nothing to do with me hating Christianity. The truth is, I AM A CHRISTIAN and that's the reason I hate this movie - because it paints Christians in the stupidest, most judgmental and one-sided light I can think of.

This movie is biased like nobody's business. All the Christians are handsome, good people, and all of the Atheists are jerks with nothing but hate in their hearts. It's terrible.

If this film was hoping to minister to Atheists and help them come to Jesus, it did exactly the opposite. Not to mention that the movie is so cliche, the dialogue is terrible, and the actors are just cringeworthy.

Once again, I'm a Christian and so the reasons I hate this film have nothing to do with religion. It's just terrible, and I find it so offensive. I'm embarrassed to have it associated with Jesus.
