Review by Rizki

Watching a serial killer doing their action is undeniably terrifying but watching them trying to forcefully form their replacement? Never imagined would be this unnerving.

Chained is such a dark, disturbing and provocative psychological thriller that explores trauma through the lens of a brutal and unsettling crime. Man, despite all the brutal things that happened in this film, this is actually really sad. The film shows how trauma can shape a person’s moral compass and turns them into the worst thing possible.

For me, this is not an easy watch. Its grim subject matter, graphic violence, and the tension between victimhood and complicity makes me feel uncomfortable in every way. It’s a morally complex narrative yet thought-provoking about its bleak and harrowing subject matter—especially trauma. The ending.. quite had me speechless. I knew something was creeping in but it’s still quite shocking the hell out of me.
