Criminal

2016

Directed by Ariel Vromen

Synopsis

The mission is in the memories.

CIA Agent Bill Pope is on a mission to track down a shadowy hacker named 'The Dutchman'. When he gets mysteriously killed, an experimental procedure transfers his memories into a dangerous ex-convict. When he wakes up Pope's memories, his mission is to eliminate The Dutchman before the hacker launches ICBMs and starts World War III.

Review by Travis Lytle

Starring a lumbering Kevin Costner as a Frankenstein's-monster made up of not-quite-equal parts psychopath and family-man-hero, "Criminal" is a surprisingly appealing science fiction film in action flick's clothing. Wearing its dark and gritty heart on its sleeve, the film is fast, violent, and pulpy fun.

Revolving around a CIA operation to track down a powerful hacker, the film finds a dead agent's memories implanted into a convict in an experiment meant to provide the intelligence organization a tool in their hunt. The story effectively combines action set-pieces with character beats for a swift and meaty narrative.
