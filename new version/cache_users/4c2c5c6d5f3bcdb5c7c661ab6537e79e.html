Review by Gregor Kreyca 8

Not as serious as part one, not as explicit as part two and not as crazy as part three, Death Wish 4: The Crackdown is more of a standard action/revenge thriller. Frequent Bronson collaborator J. Lee Thompson took over directing duties and he does a competent job. Part 4 however fails to stand out in any particular way and could therefore be considered the weakest of the bunch, yet. It’s still not a bad movie. Just a by the numbers late 80’s Charles Bronson action vehicle. Nothing overly inspired but there are worse ways to spend 99 minutes.
