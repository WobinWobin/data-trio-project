Imperium

2016

Directed by Daniel Ragussis

Synopsis

Defend your nation. Become your enemy.

Nate Foster, a young, idealistic FBI agent, goes undercover to take down a radical white supremacy terrorist group. The bright up-and-coming analyst must confront the challenge of sticking to a new identity while maintaining his real principles as he navigates the dangerous underworld of white supremacy. Inspired by real events.

Review by cinemasauron 5

For the most part, Imperium does amazingly well. Palpably tense & provocative, it really is a shame that when it was time to detonate the drama that had been ratcheting up from the start, the filmmakers decide to cop-out and take a detour instead to reach the desired destination, thus ruining the whole journey the audience invested in.

The story follows a young FBI agent who decides to go undercover to find leads on some missing cans of a radioactive material when a connection appears between its disappearance and a white supremacist group. But in order to foil whatever it is that those fanatics are planning, he first needs to immerse himself into the spiteful culture.
