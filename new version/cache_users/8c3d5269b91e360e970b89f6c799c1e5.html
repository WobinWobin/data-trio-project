Review by Lexzilla 8

Gloriously unhinged violence. On the level where Charles Bronson actually uses a bazooka to shoot a grown man out of his living room. I’m not sure it gets more entertaining than this.

Giving up all pretence of gritty reality, Death Wish 3 (not III) sees us in an urban wasteland where theatrically dressed punks are robbing, raping and murdering, in broad daylight, roughly every two minutes. The police are useless. Martin Balsam is suffering. And it’s all down to an older man with a lot of guns and a cool leather jacket to save the day.

All this builds towards a finale where anything that can explode, does explode and Bronson shoots down every bad guy whilst the good people of the wasteland holler and cheer. It’s just so much stupid fun.
