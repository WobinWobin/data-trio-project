Fred Claus

2007

Directed by David Dobkin

Synopsis

Everybody has that one relative who can't help but cause problems over the holidays. Even Santa.

Fred Claus and Santa Claus have been estranged brothers for many years. Now Fred must reconcile his differences with his brother whom he believes overshadows him. When an efficiency expert assesses the workings at the North Pole and threatens to shut Santa down, Fred must help his brother to save Christmas.

I just need to say that Paul Giamatti in the 2000s was simply out of control. In just a three year stretch, he went from playing Cleveland Heep to Santa Claus to John Adams. He was Harvey Pekar. He was Big Momma's partner. He was an ape on a whole planet of them. He got drunk on a whole lotta wine. He was a "big fat liar". He appeared in 21 theatrically released films in this decade and each role is unique to the next. The man did it all, and continues to do it all. I find it compelling that he and Philip Seymour Hoffman both came up as "that guy" character actors in the 90s at the same time and both blossomed as A-list character actors in the 00s at the same time. As for Fred Claus, I can't believe it exists but here it is.
