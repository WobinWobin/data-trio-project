This movie RULES! It remains just as exciting, impactful, funny, and magical as it was 4 years ago. The New Hope rehashing surprisingly works for me, because the script and direction evoke a very different tone to that of the original Star Wars even though they follow the same plot beats. Sometimes the movie bends a little too far backwards to justify a New Hope parallel (Starkiller Base is the lamest shit ever) but for the most part, I'm really on board with this one.

These characters are instantly lovable and relatable. Rey, Kylo, Finn, BB-8, etc. are all delights, and this movie gives me everything I could've wanted out of an older Han Solo before wrapping up his story…

Review by Sean Fennessey 17

Like all post-‘99 Star Wars movies, this one is best seen 10 years later and with a little kid you love. Still cannot believe how brilliantly they nailed the Daisy Ridley-John Boyega-Adam Driver trifecta. The final confrontation among them still raises the hairs on my arms. Harrison Ford should have been given a makeup Oscar — his work in this is his best since The Fugitive. (If Sylvester Stallone could be nominated that year for reviving his signature character, surely the infinitely more beloved Ford could have?) All the practical creatures and effects and efforts to physically rebuild Lucas’ world look and feel even better than they did upon release. The iterative nature of the story is never something I’m going to appreciate, and this was a chance for a braver version of the story that the powers that be never would have allowed for. We didn’t get that, but I like what we have.
