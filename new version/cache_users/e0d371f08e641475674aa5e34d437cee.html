Censor

2021

Directed by Prano Bailey-Bond

Synopsis

You can't edit reality.

A screener at the British Board of Film Classification (BBFC), who has earned an unsavory reputation for being the strictest censor of violent films, begins to spiral out of control after viewing a low-budget horror with similarities to the disappearance of her sister.

Review by nick 5

Censor is one of the best indie horrors I've seen in a long time. It's a full on retro, stylish and effectively skin-scrawling experience that explores the complexity of human psyche in the most "nasty" way possible. It's the type of horror that specifically caters to its core audience at the risk of driving away others who are simply not comfortable with its genre and slow-burn nature.

Censor feels like the love child of House of the Devil and In Fabric, as it in a similar manner organically borrows the ethos and blood-soaked visuals of the VHS era, while retaining an identity of its own. As a story about a film censor who finds uncanny similarities between the slasher movies…

David Cronenberg once said the censor occupies the role of the schizophrenic because their job depends on not knowing the difference between fantasy and reality.
