The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie

2004

Directed by Stephen Hillenburg

Synopsis

Hero. Legend. Sponge.

There's trouble brewing in Bikini Bottom. Someone has stolen King Neptune's crown, and it looks like Mr. Krab, SpongeBob's boss, is the culprit. Though he's just been passed over for the promotion of his dreams, SpongeBob stands by his boss, and along with his best pal Patrick, sets out on a treacherous mission to Shell City to reclaim the crown and save Mr. Krab's life.

There are few times in my life where I’m totally checked out. Where I’m free from my worries and my to-do list. Among those times is every time I’ve watched The SpongeBob SquarePants Movie.

In 2018, I saw a 35mm screening of the movie in New York City. It was just days after a devastating breakup, it felt almost foolish to be going to something so juvenile in the midst of a pain that felt so adult. Yet, lo and behold, for an hour and a half I was cackling. I wasn’t Joel who just said goodbye to his best friend, I was Joel staying up late burning out the DVD at my friend Gabe’s house in 3rd grade. Actually,…
