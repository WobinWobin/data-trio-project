The Dressmaker

2015

Directed by Jocelyn Moorhouse

Synopsis

Revenge is back in fashion.

In 1950s Australia, beautiful, talented dressmaker Tilly returns to her tiny hometown to right wrongs from her past. As she tries to reconcile with her mother, she starts to fall in love while transforming the fashion of the town.

Uh, holy shit, everyone. How come everyone isn't talking about this film, all of the time?

What initially appears to be a charmingly broad Australian comedy, in the vein of The Castle, or Strictly Ballroom, or Priscilla Queen of the Desert perhaps, gradually takes a tour through every other genre (bar maybe sci-fi), slowly morphing into what appears to be some kind of gender-switched (genre switched?) version of High Plains Drifter, with sewing machines instead of guns, and also... better.
