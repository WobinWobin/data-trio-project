Sorry, Baby

2025

Directed by Eva Victor

Synopsis

Welcome to the world.

Agnes feels stuck. Unlike her best friend, Lydie, who’s moved to New York and is now expecting a baby, Agnes still lives in the New England house they once shared as graduate students, now working as a professor at her alma mater. A ‘bad thing’ happened to Agnes a few years ago and, since then, despite her best efforts, life hasn’t gotten back on track.

Review by James (Schaffrillas)

Really wanted to love this, the performances are great, the direction is good, the story structure is excellent. I guess I just found the dialogue to be a little standard and unengaging a lot of the time, especially in comparison to how gripping the wordless nighttime driving scene was. I think the main character using humor to cope is a smart idea that usually worked, but there are some jokes that landed with a thud (my audience of mostly women was dead silent at the "We...are women" scene). Don't think I heard a single chuckle during the beating a mouse to death scene either.
Ultimately though, it is admirable in a way how refreshingly messy and honest this movie is.…
