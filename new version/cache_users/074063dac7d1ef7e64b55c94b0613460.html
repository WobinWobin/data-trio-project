Pan

2015

Directed by Joe Wright

Synopsis

Every legend has a beginning.

Living a bleak existence at a London orphanage, 12-year-old Peter finds himself whisked away to the fantastical world of Neverland. Adventure awaits as he meets new friend James Hook and the warrior Tiger Lily. They must band together to save Neverland from the ruthless pirate Blackbeard. Along the way, the rebellious and mischievous boy discovers his true destiny, becoming the hero forever known as Peter Pan.

I checked the time, assuming the film was almost over.... 43 minutes left... brutal.

This is the origin story that nobody asked for, it's pretty bad. The film plays out kind of like a mystical Indiana Jones story with Hook and Peter trying to defeat Black Beard the Pirate. This is a strong enough basic premise and the direction and production of Pan are good to above average. There are hints that this could be good, but instead it is destroyed by boring actors and a script that doesn't give them much of anything interesting to do. The result is a true slog that I very much struggled to get through.

P.S. Just making it so that all the Native Americans are played by white people doesn't fix the racism problem
