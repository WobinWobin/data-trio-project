Never gonna forget the raucous cheer in the theater when Alec Baldwin says “Ethan Hunt is the living manifestation of destiny.” Absolutely love them letting Cruise play it a little dazed after the underwater mission, and absolutely absolutely love that the big sequence they marketed the movie with, is the very first thing we see. Rebecca Ferguson is one of the best adrenaline shots this franchise has received and I hope her returning means she’s being treated better than the other women in this franchise seem to have been! This one is a small dip between the two franchise highs but any other franchise would kill to have this be considered a “dip.”

Review by James (Schaffrillas)

Something about the subdued nature of Rebecca Ferguson's performance made her character a bit hard to emotionally connect with, which resulted in this story not drawing me in quite as much. On the flip side, this is best one for Benji and his relationship with Ethan so far by a long shot. Making him the damsel in the third act was a stroke of sheer brilliance. The underwater scene is not only nail biting, it's a great showcase for how thoroughly Benji believes in Ethan even as Ethan expresses visible unease about his capabilities for the first time. Real heartwarming stuff.

I also fw the opera scene so much obviously. McQuarrie's direction is very capable and solid, I think I need to see more from him to fully understand why he got to make 4 of these though

Review by demi adejuyigbe 12

This review may contain spoilers. I can handle the truth.

So much talk about luck and chance in this one and it all feels like the franchise confronting the plot armor of Ethan Hunt’s truly impossible existence head-on– but it works. A lesser series would (and did, in its 9th installment!) try and make a dumb gag of its characters’ god-like inability to face defeat, but lamp-shading it in earnest shows so much more respect for the audience and so much more reverence for Hunt. So much so that by the time he dies, comes back, and literally screams “I am the disk,” it feels both like the least believable outcome and the most inevitable conclusion. He is the alpha and the omega. He is the MacGuffin. He’s the reason…
