Review by Joe A 24

The 1-2 combo of Rachel McAdams and Dylan O’Brien is enough to move mountains, but it’s dialed up to eleven because Raimi is stupidly good at making every situation funnier and scarier. Crash zooms, match cuts, buckets of blood & bile, vfx are a bit shoddy at times but even after 17 years of being absent from horror, Raimi’s passion for the genre still burns absurdly bright.

People say they don’t make movies like they used to, except they do, and it’s Sam Raimi making them.
