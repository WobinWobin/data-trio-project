Orlando, My Political Biography

2023

Orlando, ma biographie politique

Directed by Paul B. Preciado

Synopsis

Power to the people.

Virginia Woolf's 1928 novel "Orlando: A Biography" follows the centuries-spanning life of a young nobleman who awakens to find that they are a woman. Almost a century after its publication, Paul B. Preciado claims that fiction has become reality and Orlando's story lies at the root of all contemporary trans and non-binary life.

Review by nick

It's such a cool idea to channel non-binary and transgender ideas from the work of Virginia Woolf, and to connect queer struggles in a historical sense. Spanish trans director Paul B. Preciado created a beautiful, highly personal and unapologetically subversive essay documentary that's not without its flaws, but still charming and inspirational.

Comprising of segments where non-binary people act out the story of Orlando by Virginia Woolf, this is way more theoretical than it's plot-driven. Despite not being a filmmaker in real life, Preciado took on a more intuitive approach to get his points across, and the result is a raw but eye-opening experience. I'm not the biggest fan of the monotonous historical reenactment scenes, but those scarce moments of experimental tryouts show more potential and intrigue, for example, the brilliant operation scene.
