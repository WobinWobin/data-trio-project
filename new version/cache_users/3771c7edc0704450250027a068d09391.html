Review by Jake Cole 1

Absolutely nonsensical, looks like a fan-film uploaded to YouTube (imagine having the gall to say this ugly looking parade of DTV locations gussied up with third-rate CGI was your tribute to Blade Runner), and is insipidly provocative with casual allusions to pedophilia. There's also a disturbing streak of reflexive mockery and disgust with the genderfluid identities and style of many background characters, which I supposed you could chalk up to the protagonist's supposed religious influence, but A) that angle is handled so sloppily that you never get a sense that Leo has any feelings about religion and B) is still pretty bad considering he is depicted as the only resolutely good person in this besides the swiftly disappeared girlfriend. I know David Bowie is Jones's dad, but dedicating this half-assed sci-fi with intense homophobia to Ziggy fucking Stardust is just insulting. A new low in Netflix's war on science fiction.
