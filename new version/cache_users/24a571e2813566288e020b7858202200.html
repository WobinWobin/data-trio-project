Super-duper serious and fragmented, a textbook example of a film where the scars of a troubled production are clearly visible on screen.

90 minutes was nowhere near enough to build the world for this Stephen King adaptation, and damn does it show in an irritating, IRRITATING way. The whole thing feels tonally off, with an insanely rushed narrative, so the characters mental states and choices seem trapped in their own kind of narrative purgatory. And on top of that, the film just looks bleak and not in a good way at all.
