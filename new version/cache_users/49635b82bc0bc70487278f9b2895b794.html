As the Gods Will

2014

神さまの言うとおり

Directed by Takashi Miike

Synopsis

Oh my God, please return my tedious everyday.

High school student Shun Takahata is bored. Bored with the day-to-day monotony of school and life, he prays for change, for something exciting. Suddenly, he and his classmates are forced to play deadly children's games and facing terrifying creatures from a talking Daruma doll to a sharp-clawed lucky cat.

Review by Rafael "Mister Movie" Jovine 4

ACTION! - KILLER MIIKE

While watching Squid Game, did you ever think how awesome it would be if Takashi Miike directed it? Well, is it your lucky day, because I have the perfect movie for you! And as to be expected, it's weirder, gnarlier, and more creative. There are also some elements of fantasy, and while the CGI is far from perfect, its flaws actually add to the charm of the film. The “green, red...” game here is a Daruma doll that blows, splatting a bunch of blood, all while turning a lot of the blood too in this kind of red marble. There’s a “duck duck goose” kind of game called Kagome Kagome involving some flying Kokeshi dolls. Weird,…
