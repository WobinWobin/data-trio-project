Review by King #adoptdontshop 13

Mixed feelings.

Rob Marshall's controversial film about a girl who became the most sought after geisha in Japan felt like three seasons worth of a series was crammed into one. What's impressive about Memoirs Of A Geisha is the lavish art direction and set design, beautiful costume design, haunting music, and tight cinematography. Every artistic detail is immaculate and regal. The acting of our main cast ranges from good to strong too - but queen Gong Li is the real star here; a fiery performance seeping through the rouge and white powders on her face.

However, the cast is also Memoirs' glaring error. Casting Chinese actresses for Japanese roles is offensive, to say the least. And they're speaking in English?…
