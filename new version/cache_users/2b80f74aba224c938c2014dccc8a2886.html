Synopsis

Thank you Neil Simon for making us laugh at falling in love... again.

After being dumped by her live-in boyfriend, an unemployed dancer and her 10-year-old daughter are reluctantly forced to live with a struggling off-Broadway actor.

Forever gonna be a favorite of mine as The Goodbye Girl is a clever, warm and hilarious New York romcom with eccentric characters, jokes that land and working emotional beats. I become so interested in the relationship between Elliott and Paula that I forget I’m watching a movie. But by far the best character here is Quinn Cummings as Lucy or “the kid”. If you had a shitty day or are just in a bad mood, put this on.

Review by Maria 8

The Goodbye Girl defies uncertainty, as two accidental roommates discover certainty in each other.

Paula and Elliot are performers living in New York. She’s a dancer struggling to find work again after a two-year break and he an actor cast in an off-off-off Broadway production of Richard III. He arrives in her life one rainy night, ready to move into her apartment that he’s sublet from her actor ex-boyfriend who’s left her and her daughter Lucy for a film. Given no other choice, Paula reluctantly accepts him, and his stay is as stormy as can be. Elliot is not welcome, and neither is Paula; they make each other’s lives hell with strict rules and quirky habits. Eventually, they make peace…
