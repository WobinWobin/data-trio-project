Review by King #adoptdontshop

Ashton Kutcher and Katherine Heigl rocked action comedy movie Killers with their cute and shockingly good chemistry, but the material itself killed off the comedic vibes. 

The jokes doesn't land, the twist is obvious from a mile away, and the first arc is excruciatingly long. The action elements aren’t fine-tuned too, and the storytelling is jumpy in most places. Even veterans Catherine O’Hara and Tom Selleck failed to supply the momentum. Granted, Killers has it's own fun moments, tolerable cheese and merited goof under director Robert Luketic; Kutcher even looked like a hardened action star for a fraction of the movie, but the “husband-is-a-secret-spy” trope was used quite often and done eons better before to still be funny nor exciting in the 2010s.
