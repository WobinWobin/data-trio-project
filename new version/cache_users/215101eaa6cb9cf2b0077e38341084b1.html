Directed by Jesper Ganslandt

Lines are meant to be crossed.

Sean Haggerty only has an hour to deliver his illegal cargo. An hour to reassure a drug cartel, a hitman, and the DEA that nothing is wrong. An hour to make sure his wife survives. And he must do it all from the cockpit of his Cessna.

One-location thrillers can be brilliant (Buried, Locke) or exciting despite contrivance (Phone Booth, Wheelman), but they need to have a hook. Beast of Burden goes nowhere very slowly, despite what the music score tries to ram down your throat. I still love Dan, though.
