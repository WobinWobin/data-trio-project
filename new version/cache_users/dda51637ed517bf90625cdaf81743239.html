Synopsis

What are you prepared to do?

Elliot Ness, an ambitious prohibition agent, is determined to take down Al Capone. In order to achieve this goal, he forms a group given the nickname “The Untouchables”.

Somehow went 32 years without seeing this. Got into a long debate with my viewing companion about to what extent the movie's depiction of Eliot Ness was sincere or tongue-in-cheek, to what extent this mattered, and to what extent I was making excuses for a celebrated male auteur. Ultimately my feeling is that for De Palma, this movie is first and foremost an exercise in style, and a chance to traffic in the aesthetics and tropes of Old Hollywood gangster movies. Review by Sethsreviews 8

An overtly ridiculous and boisterous effort that tests limits of integrity in ways seen in other works, but when mixed with De Palma's classic visual brilliance, it’s a total and complete fucking blast. Costner and Connery are such an enchanting pair who play off each other so well, absolutely nailing every amusing and cathartic narrative beat. Plus, the split-diopter shots in this are to die for. Been one of my favourite De Palma’s for many many years; it's safe to say that nothing has changed.
