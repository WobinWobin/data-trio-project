Synopsis

This ignites a passion for rocket science in him and he sets out to follow his dream and escape his home town, which is basically a poverty-ridden coal mining settlement (not a fun place at all).

I've never seen a more brilliant and beautiful visual representation of what it's like to lose your dream than I did while watching October Sky. The scene is brief but powerful. Homer Hickam (Jake Gyllenhaal), a 17 year old with a dream to build a rocket to reach space, is riding an elevator shaft down into a mine for the first time while he sees a shooting star, or sputnik, flashing across the sky. The camera is stationary as he descends into the mine, so he gets further out of our sight in the frame. Each foot lower into the ground, he goes - a place where working means possibly losing your life every day - it's as if…
