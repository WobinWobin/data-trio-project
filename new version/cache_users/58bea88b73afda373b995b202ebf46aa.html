Unleashed

2005

Directed by Louis Leterrier

Synopsis

Serve no master

Raised as a slave, Danny is used to fighting for his survival. In fact, his "master," Bart, thinks of him as a pet and goes as far as leashing him with a collar so they can make money in fight clubs, where Danny is the main contender. When Bart's crew is in a car accident, Danny escapes and meets a blind, kindhearted piano tuner who takes him in and uses music to free the fighter's long-buried heart.

Review by More_Badass

Is Unleashed uneven, saggy, schmaltzy, and outrageously contrived? Oh, yes, absolutely. As a teen, I was frustrated by all the talking, and that feeling hasn’t changed much in over a decade. But does Unleashed also radiate heart and charm, feature two major powerhouse actors as dramatic counterweights to the action, and include a hefty amount of furious Yuen Woo-Ping choreography? Also, yes, absolutely.

I hadn’t seen Unleashed since my teen years. Along with The One, this was my introduction to Jet Li all those years ago. And next to The Matrix, this was among my first introductions to martial-arts cinema. So there’s a lot of nostalgia tied to this odd unassuming gem.
