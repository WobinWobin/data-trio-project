Hard Boiled

1992

辣手神探

Directed by John Woo

Synopsis

As a cop, he has brains, brawn, and an instinct to kill.

A cop who loses his partner in a shoot-out with gun smugglers goes on a mission to catch them. In order to get closer to the leaders of the ring he joins forces with an undercover cop who's working as a gangster hitman. They use all means of excessive force to find them.

Review by Will Sloan

I'll always remember watching this for the first time when I was a teenager, in my parents' basement on a bootleg DVD-R I bought in Toronto Chinatown. The hospital climax was so intense I could feel myself starting to sweat. I had never seen anything like it. Still haven't!

In this movie, it's rarely good enough to shoot someone just once - they have to get shot a dozen times, their body gyrating in space as the squibs go off. The violence in this movie is so extreme that it becomes abstracted from reality - or at least it usually does, because I have to admit that scenes of innocent bystanders getting mowed down in and around a hospital hit differently for me given what's in the news these days.

One thing you can't deny. There is a female character in this movie.
