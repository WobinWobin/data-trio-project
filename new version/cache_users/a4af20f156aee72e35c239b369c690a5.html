He's the world's coolest dad and he's gonna prove it.

A father, who can't keep his promises, dies in a car accident. One year later, he returns as a snowman, who has the final chance to put things right with his son before he is gone forever.

Review by Matt Singer

A movie about my two greatest fears:

1. Dying young and leaving my children without a father.

2. Talking snowmen.

My favorite part is when the snowman that is also the reincarnation of the kid’s dead father says to his son “Charlie, I can explain everything!”

You can? Really? I personally would love to hear this. Naturally, he does not explain everything; the movie immediately pivots to a cutesy effects-heavy snowball fight and toboggan chase. Oh well.
