Cars 3

2017

Directed by Brian Fee

Synopsis

From this moment, everything will change.

Blindsided by a new generation of blazing-fast racers, the legendary Lightning McQueen is suddenly pushed out of the sport he loves. To get back in the game, he will need the help of an eager young race technician with her own plan to win, inspiration from the late Fabulous Hudson Hornet, and a few unexpected turns.

Review by Douwe Peters 7

This film should have been the sequel. I have no idea what their intention with the sequel was. It was a fun ride but it had no reason to exist.

That all changes with Cars 3. This film is on par with the original. It's character driven, has it's focus on racing and knows it's characters (thank god Mater was put back to his roots).

Cruz is a great addition to the Cars universe. She has a great character arc and is very likable. I also like the similarities of her to McQueen and McQueen to Hudson.

The resolution of this film can be predicted from miles away but it felt true to the characters and their journey to me.…
