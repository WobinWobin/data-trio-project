Review by Sam Morrison

Sorry to all those Family Guy fans out there (which I also love, don't get me wrong) but Robot Chicken spoofed this monumental franchise better and nailed everything they poke fun at.

There are many skits here that should go down in history but possibly the greatest is Palpatine getting the call about the Death Star being blown up. No other word to use but genius.

Robot Chicken has always had a special place in my heart based on the grueling process of stop motion animation and its unbiased destruction of every popular franchise out there. If you've never seen this or the show in general, seek it out now and prepare to laugh your ass off.
