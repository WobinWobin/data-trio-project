Synopsis

Happiest. Movie. Ever.

Queen Poppy and Branch make a surprising discovery — there are other Troll worlds beyond their own, and their distinct differences create big clashes between these various tribes. When a mysterious threat puts all of the Trolls across the land in danger, Poppy, Branch, and their band of friends must embark on an epic quest to create harmony among the feuding Trolls to unite them against certain doom.

Started as something that felt like a demon wrapped in Lisa Frank stickers and it continued that way and eventually ended that way as well. 

It’s oddly surreal: some trolls have sentient tears, one troll shits out a whole birthday cake, at one point one of the characters meets God, there’s a call out post for white people erasing the musical history of black people and stealing their music, a kpop vs reggae battle... honestly nothing surprised me after 20 minutes. 

It has a wholesome message about accepting diversity and getting along. Questionable ending but the bright colors were distracting enough that I don’t care.
