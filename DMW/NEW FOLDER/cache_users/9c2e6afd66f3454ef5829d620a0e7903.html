Review by Georgia Coley 5

This is the glorious and gorgeous last gasp of 2D animation, Jack Johnson didn't have to go that hard, and etc, but something even more important: this movie ends with the Man in the Yellow Hat and Curious George blasting off in a rocket ship from a museum exhibit and making circles around the earth with the intention of being home by dinner. I really miss when kids' movies were imaginative and heightened enough to pull off stuff like that. And yes - Jack Johnson didn't have to go that hard. But he did. It's so good.
