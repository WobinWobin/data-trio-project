Synopsis

Discover the making of a king.

The story of Michael Jackson, one of the most influential artists the world has ever known, and his life beyond the music. His journey from the discovery of his extraordinary talent as the lead of the Jackson Five, to the visionary artist whose creative ambition fueled a relentless pursuit to become the biggest entertainer in the world, highlighting both his life off-stage and some of the most iconic performances from his early solo career.

What I learned from this movie:

-Michael Jackson is a perfect, flawless human being who helps sick kids, donates to charity, loves animals, loves his fans, and hates violence. That’s it. Nothing else. Don’t look into it.

-Michael’s brothers—the Jackson 5– are perfect, flawless human beings who are talented, who adore Michael, and who dislike domestic abuse. That’s it. Nothing else. Don’t look into it.

Biopic slop to the highest degree.


Edit: Unbeknownst to me, there’s going to be a sequel covering the second half of his life? If that is true, then that doesn’t excuse the obvious, eye-rolling scenes of Michael helping out kids nor does it justify the prioritization of fan service over a captivating story.
