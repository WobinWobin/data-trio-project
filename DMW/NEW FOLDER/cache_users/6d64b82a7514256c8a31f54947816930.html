Review by anna nomaly 2

The first hour, with an interminable clip show of the original film followed by a couple of routine kills, is fairly worthless in most respects. However, Eric Freeman's untrained, unfiltered, unhinged performance is the linchpin that keeps it interesting, and as soon as Ricky begins dating Jennifer, the whole thing comes to life and morphs into a Midnight Movie dream for any trash goblin worth their salt. Everyone knows "garbage day," but I promise that's only the tip of the icicle. A qualified recommendation has never before been so oddly essential.
