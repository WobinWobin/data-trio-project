I think the lesson here is that genre films really deserve more respect for being incredibly difficult to do well. Dare I say, far more difficult than your average prestige drama. 
A baffling film all around and surprisingly dull to boot. Feels like it got hacked to pieces in the reshoots and edits. Maybe there’s a coherent, satisfying version somewhere in the ether, but honestly, I kinda doubt it.
