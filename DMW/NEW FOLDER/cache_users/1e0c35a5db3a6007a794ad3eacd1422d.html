One Life

2023

Directed by James Hawes

Synopsis

Save one life, save the world.

British stockbroker Nicholas Winton visits Czechoslovakia in the 1930s and forms plans to assist in the rescue of Jewish children before the onset of World War II, in an operation that came to be known as the Kindertransport.

Review by Shreesh 3

This was a tough watch. The filmmaking here is very standard, the direction, the screenplay, the score and the editing, everything felt very textbook British biopic way, nothing innovative in terms of cinematic experience.

But I simply cannot disregard the emotional impact this film contains. The heroic journey of Nicholas Winton was highly inspiring to witness and though I wasn’t getting emotionally invested with any characters except the protagonist, I could feel the tragedy and pain felt throughout the film. Then the third act happened, I wasn’t able to hold my tears, it was heartbreaking and heartfelt. I know if my dad sees this film he will be angry, devastated and shocked, I can see elders loving this film and…

Review by MrPPeeps 2

I've never felt so useless after watching a film in all my life.

From a purely technical point of view "One Life" itself is perfectly 'fine'. However, the true story of Nicholas Winton's unbelievably selfless actions pre-WW11 is so powerful and moving, that it's immediately an important must watch part of cinema as far as I'm concerned. Any film that can evoke that much emotion from an entire theatre audience (and I mean everyone, the entire screen was silent during the credits) should receive a tonne of praise. Plus, you get to witness one of the greatest actors who has ever lived, deliver another terrific performance.
