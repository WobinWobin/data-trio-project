Ryuichi Sakamoto: CODA

2017

Directed by Stephen Nomura Schible

Synopsis

A portrait of genius music composer Ryuichi Sakamoto.

Oscar-winning composer Ryuichi Sakamoto weaves man-made and natural sounds together in his works. His anti-nuclear activism grew after the 2011 Fukushima disaster, and his career only paused after a 2014 cancer diagnosis.

Review by Inherently Viced 2

No noise is just a noise. No sound is just a sound. No music is just a music. No art is only an art, it is also an artist behind art. Ryuichi Sakamoto is a musician who seeks for something more than just a music, his music is unique and uncompromising statement about life in its many forms. He’s like a philosopher who’s trying to understand life but instead of using words, he uses sound, and by saying “sound” I mean not just music in a conventional sense of this word, but almost any sound a human or nature can produce, from rustling grass to raindrops beating against glass. Ryuichi Sakamoto is a wise rebel who eliminates all existing boundaries…
