Miroirs No. 3

2025

Directed by Christian Petzold

Synopsis

On a weekend trip to the countryside, Laura miraculously survives a car crash. Physically unhurt but deeply shaken, she is taken in by a local woman who witnessed the accident and now cares for Laura with motherly devotion. When her husband and adult son also give up their initial resistance to Laura's presence, the four of them slowly build up some family-like routine. But soon they can no longer ignore their past...

Review by Matt Neglia 2

MIROIRS NO. 3 may not be the best Christian Petzold film but his minimalist, aching examination of complex emotions and distilling them in extremely humanistic ways remains a as powerful as ever. I spent most of the film thinking something terrible was going to happen, only to realize it already had and what I was watching was far more profound and moving. Paula Beer and Petzold remain a match made in heaven and I hope they continue to make films together forever.

Review by Josh Lewis 2

This review may contain spoilers. I can handle the truth.

Don't often say this but I think Petzold was maybe a little too economical here. I think an extra 15 minutes after the reveal that this family is creepily trying to replace their dead daughter Vertigo-style (dressing her, having her stay in her room, making her play her piano, etc) with this accident victim who gets into a deadly car crash on their property would've got this to another level for me. Because it is quite beautiful and understated and I loved how subtle it is about how empty and quiet this household feels (already in the grieving/mourning process before we even enter it) and how perversely happy they are being made by recreating little household routines with this stranger…
