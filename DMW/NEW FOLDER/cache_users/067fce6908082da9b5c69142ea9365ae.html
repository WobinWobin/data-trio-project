Blade Runner 2049

2017
Rewatched 12 Jan 2025 13

Where Roy Batty represented the fear of memory being lost in time — that perhaps this may all be pointless. Joe (K) represents the beauty in leaving your mark on someone. That the human experience is truly about the impact you make on others. You don’t remember all the details of your memories, but you do remember your feelings. Those emotions are the point of it all. And so what Joe did for Deckard will remain forever with him. It will not wash away like tears in rain but leave an imprint like his indent in the snow. I’m going to go cry some more.
