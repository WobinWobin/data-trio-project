Review by Allison M. 🌱 1

I could feel the writers struggling to write the script. It was also obvious every time they were trying to be clever.

I've seen both of the leads in much better films. Supporting actress Tessa Albertson (appearing briefly as "Alex") was good, but we don't see so much of her.

The film was well shot, but some of the interesting angles didn't have much of a point and seemed just for show.

I wanted to like it far more than I did. I was hoping trolls downvoted it for no reason. But...it was just not a fine picture. After all, David Straithairn and Catherine Keener are in it too and are pretty decent. I was rooting for it to be great the whole time. But my wish did not come to fruition.

This is the 90th film I've seen with Movie Pass.
