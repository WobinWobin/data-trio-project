Directed by Davis Guggenheim

Synopsis

A short kid from a Canadian army base becomes the international pop culture darling of the 1980s—only to find the course of his life altered by a stunning diagnosis. What happens when an incurable optimist confronts an incurable disease?

Review by Matt Singer

There’s some very deft use of archival footage here, sometimes to illustrate Fox’s stories and sometimes to undercut them. One really effective sequence goes back through his various film and TV projects of the ’90s — after he was diagnosed with Parkinson’s but before he announced it to the public — and shows all the ways he tried to hide his symptoms with props and postures and various bits of physical business.

This might be seen as a compliment or a complaint, but when it was over I wanted more; more about his acting career and choices, more about dealing with his illness, more about his family and marriage, more about his philanthropy and his outlook on life now. What’s here is very good, but it does feel like there was a fair amount left undiscussed.

STILL: A MICHAEL J. FOX MOVIE contains some of the best & most seamless blending of archival & reenactment footage I’ve ever seen to create an engaging documentary experience. Michael is a wonderful subject who brings humor, optimism & awareness to his story with Parkinson’s that continues to inspire to this day. May not reveal much new information but it’s great to see him telling his own story his way.

Review by Kevflix And Chill 7

A largely nostalgic look at Michael J. Fox and a behind-the-scenes look at how his diagnosis with Parkinson’s disease affected his life and career.  Though there isn’t anything especially revelatory about this doc, it’s still fascinating to get a peak behind the curtain.  Fox didn’t make his diagnosis public for several years, initially trying to mask his condition with pills and other tricks. Seeing archive footage of him masking his left hand tremors through prop usage is illuminating. It’s a straight forward documentary, but an easy recommendation if you were a fan of his growing up, as I was.

Review by Cinema Strikes Back 🟣 2

Zu sehen, wie der Held meiner Kindheit auch heute noch so ein großes Vorbild ist, macht mich echt glücklich.

Still ist eine Dokumentation, die Michael J. Fox' Leben mit Parkinson nicht als Tragödie inszeniert, sondern stattdessen eine ermutigende Geschichte erzählt.

Es ist ein nostalgischer Einblick in die Karriere des Schauspielers und wie er damals mit der Diagnose in Zusammenhang mit seinem Beruf und seiner Familie umgegangen ist.
