The Challenges

1969
Watched 03 May 2026

Mediocre Spanish omnibus that never animates its tension much. The best episode is by far the Erice, which is the reason I saw this, but it is very tentative stuff, stronger ambiance than the earlier three and better control, but nothing that would suggest he would do Spirit of the Beehive less than 5 years later. The hook that all the episodes will move towards violence works for the most part against them, turning them into waiting games that feel straitjacketed in the need to postpone their payoffs.
