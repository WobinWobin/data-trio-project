Review by demi adejuyigbe 12

Okay, I do NOT like the way things look when they are outside or surrounded by a lot of people. All those scenes look somewhere between a nauseatingly-saturated mid-2000s boy band music video and a Robert Rodriguez-directed episode of Angela Anaconda. They also look like they were a nightmare to shoot! But the rest of it? Unimpeachable.

The Wachowskis absolutely understand how to translate a ridiculous anime to a ridiculous live-action piece without making it feel like they’re just taking the story and not the entire energy of the original. The plasticky look of the cars works so well in making the race sequences feel like a child playing with Hot Wheels, and the ghost car effect they cribbed straight…
