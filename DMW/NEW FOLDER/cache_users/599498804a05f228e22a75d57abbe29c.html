The Super Inframan

1975

中國超人

Directed by Hua Shan

Synopsis

The man beyond bionics.

The surface of the Earth is under attack, thousands of people are killed in this unprovoked attacked. The cause, Princess Dragonmon and her army of monsters have decided to invade. Princess Dragonmon is an alien whose race has been hiding under us for centuries waiting to attack at the time is right. A doctor has been preparing for something like this and turns his assistant Rayma into the cyborg hero known as Inframan. Now only Inframan stands between the Earth and Princess Dragonmon but when a close friend is captured and brainwashed, can she be stopped with this inside man feeding her info?

Review by David Whitman 4

The evil Princess Dragon Mom wants to conquer Earth. Meanwhile, Professor Chang enlists a heroic volunteer that he will transform into Infra-Man, a kung fu fighting bionic superhero. 

This is a constantly clever and supremely entertaining film. The cheapness of the suits of the various monsters (looking right out of Sid and Marty Krofft) and some of the practical special effects are what makes this film really special.

You will find vibes of Ultraman and Kamen Rider here in the best way. Just seeing Infra-Man use his special Thunderball Fist move on the baddies is worth the price of admission alone.

I could watch this film endlessly. Highly recommended.

Review by Max Ahoy 4

Hooray for the Ultra-Infra-Riderman!

I should be inclined to call this one a guilty pleasure, but I feel no semblance of shame anytime I watch it. 

Pure unbridled lunacy in its most unsullied form; infinitely more infectious but equally as addicting as any recreational drug or narcotic out there. 90 minutes spent backflipping on cloud 9 till you finally come crashing down from the most glorious cinematic high you’ll ever experience. This is my kinda cinema, this is my Citizen Kane. Thunder Ball fists, mop monsters, nipple pierced plant people, kung fu scientists, Milf Dragon Mom’s, and endless ignitable surfaces are what make what little serotonin I have left brighten my being ten fold. I will always love this subgenre; whether I’m six or sixty, anything tokusatsu/kyodai hero will always be a reminder for why life’s worth living.
