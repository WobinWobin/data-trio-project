The Only Girl in the Orchestra

2023

Directed by Molly O'Brien

Synopsis

Trailblazing double bassist Orin O'Brien never wanted the spotlight, but when Leonard Bernstein hired her in 1966 as the first female musician in the New York Philarmonic, it was inevitable that she would become the focus of much interest and fascination. Now 87 years old and recently retired, Orin looks back on her remarkable life and career, insisting that a fuss should not be made, much preferring to play a supporting role to the family, students, friends, and colleagues that surround her.

Review by Daniel

"This is my theory of how to enjoy your life incredibly - you don't mind playing second fiddle."

A documentary about Orin O'Brien, who, in 1966, became the New York Philharmonic's first female musician when Leonard Bernstein hired her as a double bassist for the orchestra ...

The Only Girl in the Orchestra is a musical documentary film directed by Molly O'Brien. It won the Academy Award for Best Documentary Short Film.

The short film, made by O'Brien's niece Molly, follows the now 87-year-old O'Brien, daughter of movie stars George O'Brien and Marguerite Churchill. It incorporates a few excerpts from interviews, as well as archive material from the 1960s and beyond, and shows her today, still teaching young bass players.

In conclusion, while The Only Girl in the Orchestra is a good portrait of its interesting subject, it ultimately feels too short, not giving the viewer enough time to really get to know Orin O'Brien.
