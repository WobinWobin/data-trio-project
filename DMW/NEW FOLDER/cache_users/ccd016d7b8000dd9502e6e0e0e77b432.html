Review by Joe A 24

Barbenheimer will force this movie into the shadows and 5 years from now Twitter will be saying, how was this not more popular??

Blaxploitation, sci-fi, comedy, satire—there’s an incredible harmony in the blending of genres by Juel Taylor. For a lot of films the genre overload would work against it, but They Cloned Tyrone uses them to weave one of this year’s most stylish and clever story. Every time you think you have it figured out, it finds a way to surprise you again. Individually— Boyega, Foxx, and Parris are operating at their best, but together? Some of the most fun you’ll have watching a movie this year.

Review by Matt! 2

This review may contain spoilers. I can handle the truth.

“Shit like this always happens to my girl Nancy Drew! Sis be coming across some weird, kinky shit that don’t make no sense, but in the end it always turns out to be some regular-degular, missionary position vanilla shit!”

Damn, this is one fun ass Get Out-esque sci-fi conspiracy thriller. Basically a blaxploitation version of They Live, anchored by the spitfire trio of John Boyega trying to distance himself as far from Finn as possible, Jamie Foxx pre-whatever the fuck health crisis he just went through, and Teyonah Parris in a role where I could watch her talk shit for hours. Gets a little heavy-handed in the back half once Kiefer Sutherland pops in to remind us he still exists, but…
