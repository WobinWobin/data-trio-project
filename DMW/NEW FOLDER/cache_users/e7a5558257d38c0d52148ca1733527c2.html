The Mastermind

2025
Watched 01 May 2026

my very first kelly reichardt film and i’m positively impressed! it’s such an interesting portrayal of a lonely man searching for something—purpose, maybe, or maybe there’s more. he’s great at what he does (quiet, observant, resolute) yet he fails at connecting with the rest—whether that’s his family, his friends, or a clearer path forward. he lies, he steals, he keeps on trying to get away with whatever he wants, and you just can’t take your eyes off him—whether it is because of the unpredictability of it all, his seemingly collected demeanor, or josh o’connor’s ability to make you stay glued to the screen.
