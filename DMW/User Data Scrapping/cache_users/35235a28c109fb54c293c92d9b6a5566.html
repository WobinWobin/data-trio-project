Bāhubali: The Beginning

2015

బాహుబలి:ద బిగినింగ్

Directed by S. S. Rajamouli

Synopsis

The young Shivudu is left as a foundling in a small village by his mother. By the time he’s grown up, it has become apparent that he possesses exceptional gifts. He meets the beautiful warrior princess Avanthika and learns that her queen has been held captive for the last 25 years. Shividu sets off to rescue her, discovering his own origins in the process.

Review by Michael James 10

No matter what, Baahubali franchise will always be remembered, as this is right where the Indian film industry took a major paradigm shift, breaking the regional barriers and redefining the new age commercial model for so called pan-Indian movies. S.S.Rajamouli 🙌

An ambitiously epic fictional period action extravaganza, that stands out for its immersively elaborate world building, spectacularly choreographed action sequences, goosebump loaded storytelling and brilliantly detailed execution. The grandeur visuals and fantastic musical score, add to the epicness. Casting was pitch perfect; Prabhas, Rana, Ramyakrishnan, Satyaraj and the entire cast were terrific. Though it has some flaws, the awe inspiring memorable moments overshadow them and leaves you on an unforgettable suspenseful cliffhanger note "Why did Kattappa kill Baahubali?",making you eagerly look forward towards the final showdown.

Review by braden 5

“My son has come! Baahubali has returned!”

I watched this on the recommendation of someone who insisted this was amazing and honestly they really weren’t wrong. Bāhubali: The Beginning is a hilariously over-the-top, action packed epic that’s just a total blast despite being nearly three hours. The plot constantly throws unexpected curves and weird moments, from the protagonist swimming underwater to secretly tattoo another character’s hand while she sleeps, to occasional out of nowhere musical sequences where characters sing, to a flashback you would assume will be brief backstory taking well over an hour and forming the film’s climax. It’s super confusing at times and a lot of its appeal comes from being ‘so bad it’s good’, but at the same time so much about it is genuinely great, and either way it’s just complete fun and entertainment from start to end.

Review by {Todd} 1

"Baahubali! Baahubali! Baahubali! Baahubali!" -Crowd of Slaves,

I want to run my fingers through Prabhas's mullet adjacent hair.

I absolutely love this movie. It's my thing, I'm into it, and I have trouble understanding why others wouldn't love this but to each their own. I'm a fan of epics in general and this is one of the more dramatic and fun epics I've watched in some time. Baahubali is like Hercules, Spartacus and James Bond all mixed together. This is a dude who can save a child and catch a spear that's been thrown at him at the same time... WHILE riding horseback at a full gallop. All the actors are charismatic, the direction is bold and dramatic with nearly every choice working, and the action is tons of fun. I appreciated the story quite a bit, though it's pretty basic, and the film is a visual delight.

I can't believe I get to watch the sequel now.
