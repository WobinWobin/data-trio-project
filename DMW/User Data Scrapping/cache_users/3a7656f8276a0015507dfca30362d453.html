He starts seeing monstrous forms prowling around all over the city... Is Angelino losing his mind, or could an alien invasion really be happening this quietly...?

Review by Dylan van Frankfoort 5

Anarchic French comics X Japanese animation. Fucking bangs, with action of the Johns Wick and Woo sort, and that distinct sense of cool you'll find in stuff like Banlieue 13 and early Bessons (without the creepiness of the latter... OK, maybe a little). You know it's cool because the Abbott himself, RZA blessed it with his voice, quoting Shakespeare while his character dual-wields belt-fed machine guns. Music to raise the dead, too, really cleverly done—at one point there's a car chase involving an ice cream truck, and its looping jingle (not that one with racist origins, thankfully, nor RZA's reimagining, which would have been awesome) is perfectly mixed with the thumping electronic score.

I don't know. The thing had me when the fridge with the Pride sticker crushed a fascist cop, but with the introduction of the Earth-defending league of wrestlers, I'm inclined to say this is the best movie I've ever seen.
