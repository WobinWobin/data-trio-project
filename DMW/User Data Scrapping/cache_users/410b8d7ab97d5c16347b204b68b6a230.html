Review by Gregor Kreyca

After “Reign of Terror” aka. “The Black Book” this was the second Period Film Noir from director Anthony Mann. That is a Film Noir movie that is set in a historical period, rather than the 1940s and 1950s. This one also falls into another sub-genre, the "Train Movie". It deals with a detective played by Dick Powell (and ironically named John Kennedy) trying to prevent an assassination attempt at Abraham Lincoln. I have to give it to Mann, he really picked interesting and unique subject matters for his Noir’s.

I would count “The Tall Target” among Mann’s better Noir movies. I like movies set on a Train and this one uses the setting well. It’s a tight and tension filled little movie that thrilled and entertained me for the most part. It would also make for a cool “Period Film Noir”-themed double feature with Mann’s Reign of Terror. So, another solid recommendation from me.

Anthony Mann’s The Tall Target is a wonderful example of how a terrific film can be made on a low budget with a so-so cast. We know from the beginning that New York cop John Kennedy (Dick Powell) will keep the newly elected Abe Lincoln (Leslie Kimmell) from being assassinated, but the way Mann and screenwriters Art Cohn and George Worthington Yates establish and maintain the suspense is sublime.
