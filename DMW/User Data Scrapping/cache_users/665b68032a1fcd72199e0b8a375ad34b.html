The Aviator

2004

Directed by Martin Scorsese

Synopsis

Some men dream the future. He built it.

A biopic depicting the life of filmmaker and aviation pioneer Howard Hughes from 1927 to 1947, during which time he became a successful film producer and an aviation magnate, while simultaneously growing more unstable due to severe obsessive-compulsive disorder.

Review by Neil Bahadur 7

Not the best, but maybe my favorite Scorsese - almost Greek mythology with Wagnerian scope, one of the best films on the correlation between ambition and madness, Icarus flying too high into the sky and crashing - many times we see the sun obliterate the rest of Scorsese's frame in the flying sequences. Also Scorsese's mastery of music is at its best here - I'm thinking precisely of the arrangements of the Howard Shore's score for the flying sequences, the ominous drones portend doom early on, from the first time we see Hughes make a plane innovation. Real life: living in the moment, making movies, being in love, and losing ones mind. Of course Hughes' dream plane is called the Hercules..
