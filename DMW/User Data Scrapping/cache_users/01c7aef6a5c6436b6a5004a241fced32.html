His Girl Friday

1940

Directed by Howard Hawks

Synopsis

They're at each other's throats when they're not in each other's arms!

Walter Burns is an irresistibly conniving newspaper publisher desperate to woo back his paper’s star reporter, who also happens to be his estranged wife. She’s threatening to quit and settle down with a new beau, but, as Walter knows, she has a weakness: she can’t resist a juicy scoop.

Review by 🇵🇱 Steve G 🐝 23

This review may contain spoilers. I can handle the truth.

I can understand why His Girl Friday is usually labelled, first and foremost, a screwball romantic comedy. There's certainly plenty going on in its last 20 minutes to support that labelling especially.

But on this, my third viewing, I still remain resolutely unconvinced that this is a romantic comedy at all. Sure, Rosalind Russell does end up following Cary Grant out the door for a honeymoon in Albany but it's not because, all of a sudden, she's been bowled over by his charms all over again. There's absolutely no sign at all until the last scene that she has changed her feelings towards him.
