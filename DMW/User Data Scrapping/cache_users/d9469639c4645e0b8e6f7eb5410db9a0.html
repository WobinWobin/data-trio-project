Melody Time is a series of vignettes with cool animation and Disney style music... it is a "modern" pretelling of Fantasia with many of the themes focusing on late 1800s, early 1900s folk tales like Johny Appleseed and the little engine that could. The animation is fantastic and the blending of score and music is exceptional. My biggest problem with the film is that the vignettes are really hit and miss, making the film quite boring occasionally. In addition, there is some occasional old school Disney racism in the movie and that's always a downer.

Review by Silent J 2

FINALLY! I'M DONE WITH THESE FUCKING PACKAGE FILMS!

Yes, this is the final Disney package film before they went on to make more narrative driven feature length films. I liked them at first, but I'll admit I got a tad sick of them after a while. Luckily, this is the last one but at least it's an enjoyable one. Like Make Mine Music, it's just a collection of shorts. Some longer than others. Some better than others. Some enjoyable.
