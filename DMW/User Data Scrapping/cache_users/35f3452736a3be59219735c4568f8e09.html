Review by Matt Neglia 1

Colman Domingo is astounding in RUSTIN. His charisma, sensitivity & power drives the film to many rousing moments that earned many strong audience reactions. It’s the performance of a lifetime in an isolated biopic told respectfully by George C. Wolfe with emotional clarity & dignity. The wheel isn’t necessarily being reinvented here but Domingo’s work, along with the rest of the ensemble makes it a compelling watch.
