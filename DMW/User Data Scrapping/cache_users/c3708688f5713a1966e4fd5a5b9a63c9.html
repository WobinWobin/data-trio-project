GetEven

1993

Directed by John De Hart, James Paradise

Synopsis

Will Justice Win Out Over Evil? Will Courage Overcome Corruption?

The Corruption of The System is given a swift lopsided kick by Vigilante Justice as a Disgraced Former Cop emerges from exile, automatic weapons blazing and mediocre martial arts akimbo, to bring down a murderous satanic cult headed by his former partner. His only allies? A boatload of swagger and one leggy blonde.

Review by Justin LaLiberty 1

There's a scene in this where Wings Hauser opens up a stack of bills individually over a toilet and shoots each one with a handgun, which is very much a *mood*. You could reductively label this as some sort of PM Entertainment-esque version of Road House but it's obviously the product of a very singular vision - even if that vision primarily occupied with live honky tonk music, Huckleberry Finn rants, gratuitous topless women and scenes that are lit by CRT TV static. So, pretty much 100% for me (and probably you too if we're friends).
