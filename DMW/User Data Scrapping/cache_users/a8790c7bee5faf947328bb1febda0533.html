Love, Simon

2018

Directed by Greg Berlanti

Synopsis

Everyone deserves a great love story.

Everyone deserves a great love story, but for 17-year-old Simon Spier, it's a little more complicated. He hasn't told his family or friends that he's gay, and he doesn't know the identity of the anonymous classmate that he's fallen for online.

"You took that away from me."

What I love most about Love, Simon is how it so explicitly demonstrates the difference between being being proud of who you are and being ready to say it. Identity is personal, and it is no one's right to demand that we reveal ourselves. Simon never doubts he's gay, nor does he wish he wasn't, but he just isn't ready to be seen differently. I've seen this concept explored so rarely, even within the world of independent film.
