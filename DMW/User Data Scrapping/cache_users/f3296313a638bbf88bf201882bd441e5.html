Review by Alice Stoehr 2

You know the iconic poster for this movie, with a giant woman sultrily terrorizing highway traffic? It's maybe a little bit misleading. This is really a James M. Cain-esque desert noir wherein a scumbag gaslights his alcoholic heiress wife. Gradually, sci-fi elements intrude, with special effects so crummy and low-budget they approach the avant-garde. A translucent giant, for example, wears a costume obviously left over from some medieval adventure movie and rides around in a spherical, diamond-powered spaceship. Once the radiation from his touch has enlarged the poor heiress, she's represented through an echoing voice and a manacled, Claes Oldenburg-style papier-mâché hand.

Later, her rampage through town is accomplished with a series of double exposures superimposing an actress—possibly a body…
