Be Kind Rewind

2008

Directed by Michel Gondry

Synopsis

You name it, we shoot it.

A man whose brain becomes magnetized unintentionally destroys every tape in his friend's video store. In order to satisfy the store's most loyal renter, an aging woman with signs of dementia, the two men set out to remake the lost films.

Review by Sethsreviews 4

Such an odd film, but rewardingly charming in lots of ways. you’ve got just the most random dynamic of Jack Black and Mos Def at the helm, but it works (granted, this was Jack Black’s good-era). functions as a tale of legend, highlighting the significance of preserving artists via reflection and repair. exists because of the passion for tangible media: ironically, I actually remember renting this from Blockbuster right before everything fell apart. Michael Gondry is often seen as one of the typical one-hit wonder directors, but I reflect on him as more of a two-hit kinda guy. Just lovely stuff!
