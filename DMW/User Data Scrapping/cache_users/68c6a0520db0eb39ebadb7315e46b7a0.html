The Lady from Shanghai

1947
Watched 03 May 2026

Orson Welles might sound like a leprechaun, but man, The Lady from Shanghai is all that and then some. 

Rita Hayworth wrote the book on how to put the femme in Femme Fatale. My word, she is everything you could ever ask for: Hot, smokin’ hot and hotter.

I am still trying to figure out why Grisby sounded like Sylvester the Cat? But, damn, he is such a menace.

Welles was such a brilliant mind and filmmaker. The funhouse mirrors scene is beyond words. A must watch for fans of Noir.
