Review by Brett Schutt 1

This is lowkey a really good movie and easily the best TV movie based off a show that probably exists.

It's really well written, incredibly funny, kinda heartfelt.

Does it look a little cheap? Sure. Does it feel a little rushed? Maybe. Does that matter? No, not really.

It's a perfect film to shut your brain off and watch the silliness, while still getting genuine smart humor and heart to spare.

I miss when Nickelodeon actually made good shows. Drake and Josh was my childhood.
