Review by Kevflix And Chill 4

Probably the most disappointing of my Sundance 2023 slate. Jennifer Connelly stars as a former child actress that has checked herself into a wellness retreat run by Elon (name choice 🤮) played by Ben Whishaw. Connelly and Whishaw’s scenes together are probably some of the best the film has to offer. And when Connelly adamantly professes “I did not poop!” during one of the role playing sessions, was easily the funniest moment. Aside from that, the film just didn’t really amount to much for me. Dark comedy, I know, is a tough thing to pull off well.  I had probably set expectations unfairly high for Alice Englert’s (Jane Campion’s daughter) debut feature.

Review by Nick 2

“You’re not a bad person, just bad behaviour.” 

There’s things to like here no doubt (especially considering this is Englert’s debut feature) but ultimately this movie just wasn’t for me, maybe it just went way over my head. 

The highlight of this though is assuredly its lead actors who deliver such tortured performances, especially Connelly. But as stellar & profound as the lead performances are, what took me out of this the most was the pacing. This just moved way too slowly for me to be fully invested in it consistently. 

The dialogue came across a little pretentious to me at times too but that also might’ve been the point. Also, talk about a strange concept for a retreat. The use…
