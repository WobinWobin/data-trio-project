Review by James (Schaffrillas) 3

For all of its famous and well-documented faults, it's impossible to deny how thematically ambitious and rich this film is as a standalone story and a piece of the larger Alien franchise. Undeniably pretentious and heavy-handed with many illogical moments to be found, but weirdly beautiful and captivating at the same time. As grand from a directorial standpoint as you would hope, with enough heart pounding sequences and existential dread to overcome my initial skepticism of how much I could really get out of a mostly Xenomorph-less Alien movie. For being one of the most divisive blockbusters of the 2010s, I definitely wasn't expecting to come out of this one on the side of its fans. I think it's pretty good! Who knew?
