Review by Dustin Mason 2

People keep saying this is a "Boogie Nights" ripoff, but this is really in the vein of "Pain and Gain". This is another fucked up true story given the raunchy cinematic treatment. First time feature director Lucas Heyne(who previously directed a Lil Romeo music video) turns in an audacious debut to say the least. I also want to point out that even though the first 55 minutes is an often hilarious black comedy, and the last half is a straight up depressing (yet effective) drama, Heyne nails both tones. Those tones don't especially gel well, but he definitely shows promise as an eclectic director who can either make the next "Observe and Report" or MAYBE the next "American Psycho".

Review by Britt W

Sundance #12

This is going to rub so many people the wrong way because it's funny, uncomfortable, revealing and a fucking emotional rollercoaster on a tragedy that actually occurred.

I appreciate this film for what it is - and it's simply a disection of a guy who wanted to rise to stardom In the porn industry when he could not handle it mentally. Additionally, it exploits how terrible people are treated in this industry, and it doesn't shy away from the graphics or dialogue.

There's really no censorship here, folks, so if you cannot handle that, skip it. But for these reasons, I understand and appreciate everything it told, and was moved by this tragic story.
