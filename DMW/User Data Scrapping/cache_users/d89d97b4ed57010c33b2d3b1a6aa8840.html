It’s a film about faith, and the pivotal role that it plays in one man’s search for meaning amidst the chaos of existence. Wrong! I love this movie. I think everything single thing about it is absolutely wonderful. A gorgeous and truly joyful farce about communists and the studio system era that feels like it celebrates each just as much as it admonishes them.
