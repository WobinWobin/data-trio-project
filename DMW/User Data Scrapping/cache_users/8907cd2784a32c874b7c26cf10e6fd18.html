Review by Joe A 4

I think I’ve finally accepted that I’m a major Bradley Cooper fan. At least in the way that I personally really gravitate to his directing and storytelling sensibilities. The constant claustrophobic close ups of Dern and Arnett, the shaky camera, the hopeful melancholy of the script, it all works for me. Arnett is essentially doing Bojack Horseman which works both because Arnett is (not so?) secretly good at being vulnerable but also because Cooper is doing everything to fill his frames with these incredibly flawed people. Probably borders being saccharine but I find honest attempts at trying to understand one another tends to feel that way. Sleeper hit of the year for me, will probably love it even more a second time.
