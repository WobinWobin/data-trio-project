Yona of the Dawn

2014

暁のヨナ

Directed by Kazuhiro Yoneda, Fumiya Houjou …

Synopsis

Currently, Hiryuu Palace has no one else next in line for the throne other than the fifteen year old princess, Yona, who had been raised with care. Finally, the night of her sixteenth birthday arrives. She expects it to be a wonderful day spent with her peace-loving father, her servant and friend Hak, and her cousin Soo-won, who she had feelings for... However... That night, Yona goes to visit her father to tell him how she really feels, because he opposes to her getting married to Soo-won. However, when she gets to her room, she encounters a shocking truth.

Review by Jay

Criminal that this doesn’t have a second season! This is what fantasy is all about! I need to find where the manga picks up at from where season 1 leaves off at because this is incredible with its world building and characterization. The blend of both the fantasy and shoujo genre work to this shows adventures to explore the constant shift of gender norm expectations within a political environment that surprisingly demands going against norms to obtain power. It’s incredibly fascinating how certain norm expectations are needed and expected in regards to going outside of the box of conformity, but characters decided to or not to cross that threshold too much is what really made me engaged with how distinct these characters are. Tina’s growth is phenomenal and I can’t wait to read the manga!
