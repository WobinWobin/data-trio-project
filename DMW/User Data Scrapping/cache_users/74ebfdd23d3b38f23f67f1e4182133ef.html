Green Book

2018

Directed by Peter Farrelly

Synopsis

Inspired by a True Friendship.

Tony Lip, a bouncer in 1962, is hired to drive pianist Don Shirley on a tour through the Deep South in the days when African Americans, forced to find alternate accommodations and services due to segregation laws below the Mason-Dixon Line, relied on a guide called The Negro Motorist Green Book.

Review by adrian 11

A road trip film? Check.

That runs through Christmas time? Check.

Viggo Mortensen in a hilarious role; Mahershala Ali being suave as all hell; the most charming on-screen pairing in recent memory?

Check, check, and check.

People will argue how Green Book could have dug deeper into its themes of race and adversity that came with its setting.

It definitely could have, but I feel other films are better suited for that; Farrelly chose to focus on themes of camaraderie, development, learning and loving, all with delightful results.

It’s perfect for what it is, and there’s something for everyone to love.
