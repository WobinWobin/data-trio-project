October (Ten Days that Shook the World)

1928
Watched 03 May 2026

"The 'traitors'" - Title Card

- THS Losers: boxd.it/iDXDC

Sergei Eisenstein’s October: Ten Days That Shook the World feels less like watching a film and more like being thrown into history at full speed. It’s chaotic, overwhelming, and deliberately disorienting, but that’s the point, the revolution isn’t meant to feel neat. The montage is still astonishing, especially in how it turns symbols into weapons. Some stretches feel more like political argument than drama, but the sheer visual power is undeniable. A film that doesn’t just depict revolution, it tries to make you feel it.
