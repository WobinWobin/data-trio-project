Zoolander

2001

Directed by Ben Stiller

Synopsis

3% body fat. 1% brain activity.

Clear the runway for Derek Zoolander, VH1's three-time male model of the year. His face falls when hippie-chic Hansel scooters in to steal this year's award. The evil fashion guru Mugatu seizes the opportunity to turn Derek into a killing machine. It's a well-designed conspiracy and only with the help of Hansel and a few well-chosen accessories like Matilda can Derek make the world safe for male models everywhere.

Review by Josh Lewis 7

You know, the term "masterpiece" gets tossed around a lot these days but...

I don't know what it says about me that there probably isn't a single movie I find funnier than this. I can't even defend or articulate exactly why beyond I find it's single satirical joke—Hollywood superficiality/vanity (a subject that between this and Tropic Thunder Stiller clearly knows a lot about) deliriously funneled through an MK Ultra conspiracy—so good that I don't care that it mostly just repeats it over and over again. The "freak gasoline fight accident" sequence is one of the greatest gags ever, and these might be my favorite Owen Wilson and Will Ferrell performances.
