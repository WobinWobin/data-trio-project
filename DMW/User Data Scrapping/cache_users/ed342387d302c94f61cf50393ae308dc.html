Philadelphia

1993

Directed by Jonathan Demme

Synopsis

No one would take on his case... until one man was willing to take on the system.

Two competing lawyers join forces to sue a prestigious law firm for AIDS discrimination. As their unlikely friendship develops, their courage overcomes the prejudice and corruption of their powerful adversaries.

as a queer man, this ripped my fucking heart out and made me cry harder than i can even describe. i can't even say how affecting and how perfect this is, i don't have the words for it. saw it for the first time about 5-6 years ago, when i was a child and not aware of my sexuality, my queerness, and it broke me completely, i sobbed over it for days. and seeing it now, as an openly bisexual man, i broke down in tears. it has such empathy and compassion, such beauty and humanity that it's impossible not to feel overwhelmed with emotion. i can't even describe how much this hit me. demme was the master of humanity, one of the greatest filmmakers of all time, and i'm so grateful to him for making this. miss him everyday.
