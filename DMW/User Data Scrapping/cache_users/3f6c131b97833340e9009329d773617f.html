Dope

2015

Directed by Rick Famuyiwa

Synopsis

It's hard out here for a geek.

Malcolm is carefully surviving life in a tough neighborhood in Los Angeles while juggling college applications, academic interviews, and the SAT. A chance invitation to an underground party leads him into an adventure that could allow him to go from being a geek, to being dope, to ultimately being himself.

Review by Alex 3

Dope is, to be blunt, a thematically confusing and annoying mess. It constantly moves from comedy (that isn't very funny), to drama (that isn't very dramatic), with almost no regard for its audience's intelligence. It has its moments, and is made with such confidence and exuberance that at times you just go with it. But when it was all said and done, I was left disappointed with the film and surprised by the almost unanimous praise for it by critics and the Letterbox community.
