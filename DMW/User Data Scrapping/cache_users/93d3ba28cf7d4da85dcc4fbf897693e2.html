Six-String Samurai

1998

Directed by Lance Mungia

Synopsis

Vegas Needs a New King.

In a post-apocalyptic world where the Russians have taken over a nuked USA and Elvis is king of Lost Vegas, Buddy is a '50s rocker and wandering warrior rolled into one, too-cool package. Armed with his six-string in one hand and his sword in the other, Buddy is on his way to Vegas to succeed Elvis as King. Along the way, he saves an orphan who decides to tag along.

Very charming in the same way that watching a high school production of a musical is charming. I don't mean to be condescending, I've acted in way lower budget stuff than this. They say you can kill 200 people and play a mean six string at the same time. 

I do not like rock ‘n roll music! It is too loud! No more rock ‘n roll music in Vegas! 

It’s been a minute since I played, but Six-String Samurai reminded me of a rockabilly Borderlands. A deluge of too-cool-for-school one-liners follow Buddy and the orphan he picked up on his way to Vegas (much like Zatoichi). The action choreography is surprisingly good for a low budget genre mashup, and the cinematography is incredibly striking - way above the pay grade. The Vinegar Syndrome UHD does the film justice, and the love is felt from the packaging to the extras. An incredible treat.

Review by Gary K

I'd never heard of this bizarro post apocalyptic, samurai, Desperado, amalgam of about a million different things in a blender with a kid's movie sensibility.

Honestly, I would have probably LOVED this if it leaned into the blood and sleazy side of things. As is, this is fun in the vein of something I would have loved as a kid if I found it on TV on a saturday afternoon while I was hopped up on sugar after the cartoons ended. Still, I found my mind wandering during certain sequences, though before I could actually get bored (or grab my phone to scroll) they'd jump to a new sequence. Still, I was never fully engaged.

Certainly one of a kind and the thing looks and sounds great in the new 4K from Vinegar Syndrome. Just not really my jam at the end of the day.
