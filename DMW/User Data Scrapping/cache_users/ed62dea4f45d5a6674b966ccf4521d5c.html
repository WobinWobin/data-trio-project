Iced

1989
Watched 02 May 2026

The Last Drive-In: Joe Bob’s Cold Cruel Christmas

Falls into the category of the Joe Bob Briggs segments being more entertaining than the film he’s presenting.

Made during the bizarre ski movie boom of the 80s. Bad dialogue and acting is nothing new in slasher films, but this one has long stretches where that’s the only thing happening. The film is so uneventful that the 14 plus hours that the In Search of Darkness documentary series spends covering almost every 80s horror films made, skips this one entirely. And they featured Hobgoblins.
