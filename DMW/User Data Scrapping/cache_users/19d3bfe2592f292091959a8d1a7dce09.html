Synopsis

In modern-day Helsinki, two lonely souls in search of love meet by chance in a karaoke bar. However, their path to happiness is beset by obstacles – from lost phone numbers to mistaken addresses, alcoholism, and a charming stray dog.

Review by Filipe Furtado 2

It is very comforting to know as the world turns to shit that we can still from time to time count on one of those Kaurismaki working class love stories. This one of his best, like a Borzage movie that happens to include a lot of laughs. That two people are supposed to be together is established by an exchange of gazes, the world will conspire to keep them separated by ways cosmical and practical, but auteurial hand if not God will intervene. Somehow the large obstacles go perfectly with Kaurismaki’s usual barebones approach and every small detail he decides to focus one is a winner. In the meantime until the couples reunion, there are lots of wonderful faces, karaoke and a great dog named Chaplin. The biggest movies help make life worth it we’ve get in a while.
