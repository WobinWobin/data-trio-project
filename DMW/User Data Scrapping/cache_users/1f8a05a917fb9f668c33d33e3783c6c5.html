It's a wonderful fuck you to everybody who glorifies the complicated dead. England lionized and propagandized this bizarre imperialist and David Lean made his fascination and co-opting of the Arab rebellion seem literally insane, dangerous, and stupid, which in itself is awesome.

Four glorious hours of camels walking in extreme long shot. Review by Jacob Knight

Always thrown by how incredibly melancholy all four hours of this truly is - beginning with the literal death of a legend as we are then forced to witness one man’s breaking point and greatest regrets. Spoilers (I guess), but T.E. Lawrence’s happy-go-lucky charmer is nowhere to be found by the final reel, as he becomes the very human embodiment of white colonialism and arrogance, thinking he can just swoop in and solve a culture’s problems while simultaneously adopting their identity. In the end, he’s truly a man without a country; a great adventurer with no home to return to, as he helped empower those he once thumbed his nose at, while watching children die and even finding he has a taste for murder. Brilliantly crushing, morose stuff, all lensed by arguably the greatest craftsman of “they don’t make ‘em like this anymore” cinematic epics. [70mm]
