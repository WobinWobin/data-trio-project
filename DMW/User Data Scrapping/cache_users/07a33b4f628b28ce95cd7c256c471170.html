Hard Ticket to Hawaii

1987

Directed by Andy Sidaris

Synopsis

Pay the price for paradise!

A Molokai-based civilian pilot and an undercover DEA agent intercept a delivery of diamonds intended for druglord Seth Romero. Seth, his henchmen, and other island undesirables launch a full-scale assault on the duo. If they're going to survive, they'll need the help of agent Rowdy Abilene and his partner/kickboxing expert Jade.

Review by Austin Shermer 6

Things I've learned from Hard Ticket to Hawaii

1. Make sure to put snakes that have been contaminated by toxic chemicals in crates that are marked "contaminated snake", not just "snake".

2. If you're guarding an area never put down your Uzi to play frisbee with a passerby. They will probably kill you with a razor-bladed frisbee.

3. DEA uniforms consist of short shorts and cut-off shirts. The short shorts which at first glance may seem impractical, do contain pockets for convenient shuriken storage.

4. Never illegally import diamonds via RC helicopter. It is not the most reliable transportation method.

5. Rocket launchers make excellent hunting weapons.

6. Boobs in every 3rd or 4th scene are an integral part of a film's plot structure.

The list could go on and on. I really wish films like this could still be made without any intended irony.
