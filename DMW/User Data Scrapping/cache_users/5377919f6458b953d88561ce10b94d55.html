FIGHT! - THE LOUDEST LAUGH: CARREY VS. SANDLER

I just finished this movie, and I honestly don't know what to say. I didn't hate it, but I don't think I liked it either. The action leaves a lot to be desired. A running gag became tiresome very quickly, and none of the humor landed.

Sandler and Spade, not surprisingly, have decent chemistry, but neither is operating at his best. This was supposed to be, or at least to feature, action, but none of it left much of an impression on me. Ultimately, the film was underwhelming; by the end I felt completely underwhelmed.
