Too often it feels like a broad summary of Ann Lee’s life. I wanted to root harder for her, feel that Holy Spirit she felt, or be let into the darker shades of this character/movement that the eerie music and cinematography often implies.

It is an interesting chapter of history with incredible craft work across the board, and the song/dance sequences are riveting. But the film’s perspective on her feels surface level.
