Ronnie O'Sullivan: The Edge of Everything

2023

Directed by Sam Blair

Synopsis

Charting Ronnie O'Sullivan's rise to fame from his early teens and the mental challenges he has overcome in the pursuit of perfection.

Ronnie O’Sullivan hasn’t had the most harmonious relationship with the media over the years, but his attempt to win the Snooker World Championship for a record 7th time in 2021 apparently proved too good an opportunity to pass up.

Review by holas79

This documentary tries incredibly hard to work out what makes Ronnie O'Sullivan firstly, such an exciting player but also such a mercurial person. Full of natural ability, he has always been a much more interesting player than his contemporaries - not afraid of playing left handed, or taking on a seemingly impossible shot. The Edge of Everything also tries to work out why he seems to put quite so much pressure on himself, delving into his battles with addiction, and his father's incarceration. His seventh World Snooker Championship victory is played out here, and although the real drama is on the table, this tries to add more by showing O'Sullivan backstage reassessing his performance after each session. Despite the win, there's no satisfying outcome; O'Sullivan continues to be a contradiction, having a love-hate relationship with the sport that seems to attract and repel him in equal measure.
