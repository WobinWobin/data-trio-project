Magellan

2025
Watched 03 May 2026

I’ve been excited to see this for a while, and it mostly lived up to the hype. I enjoyed the very deliberate, calming pacing as it approaches its destination. I thought most scenes of nature and general minimalism were nice touches. Occasionally, I lost track of what was going on, but I believe I still understood it. It’s a very grand and distressing film with a great ending. I am SO excited for whenever Beatriz The Wife comes out, as that actress was so captivating in her few scenes. Much easier to watch than I thought, but also more beautifully surreal. A very intriguing film.
