The strange trick of Chainsaw Man is that while it is a story that involves big, violent fights, it is really mostly about a kid who doesn't understand the world. Denji grew up deprived of everything, and now he is in the big city, hanging around all these people who actually seem to like him. Of course, a lot of them just want something from him -- his heart, which in a classic Fujimoto style big dumb anime metaphor that just kind of works, is also a devil.
