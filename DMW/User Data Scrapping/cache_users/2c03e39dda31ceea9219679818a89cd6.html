The Ladykillers

2004

Directed by Ethan Coen, Joel Coen

Synopsis

The greatest criminal minds of all time have finally met their match.

An eccentric, if not charming Southern professor and his crew pose as a band in order to rob a casino, all under the nose of his unsuspecting landlord – a sharp old woman.

“It was more than ass, it was literature.”

One of the goofiest Coen offerings ever offered. Did Joel take a nap and just let Ethan take the wheel here? It's possible, as this is the first Coen film in which they had separate directing credits.
