Review by Hallie 💃

David Gumpel describes this film as "incredibly violent, but otherwise pointless" and I completely agree. It's a masterpiece in its own right, because it does exactly what it sets out to do with absolute perfection. A commentary on violence in the media, aimed at American audiences, displaying a film that shows non-stop psychological torment but cuts away at every moment where anything is shown. The fourth wall breaks and somewhat sarcastic remarks thrown at the audience really drive home the point even harder. Shots go on for so long and everything is so painfully gratuitous. Nothing about this is enjoyable. I almost hated it. But that's the point. It's a good message to send, and having recently spent a lot of my time watching more violent films, holy shit did that make this hit home even harder. Great performances and all the technical stuff obviously. This is a perfect film, through-and-through. And I hate it. Unrateable.
