A letdown in every sense. What should have been a fast-paced, gloriously entertaining exhibition of star power becomes a squandered opportunity. It's just too tame to capture the delicate balance of making the spirit of awards season something awful enough for us to laugh at while also making it frenzied, chaotic, and exciting enough for us to laugh with. Even though the actors are clearly enjoying themselves in their roles, none of them could redeem this for me. Disappointing.

Review by Jay 👽 1

There's some fun to be had in For Your Consideration, but the misfit troupe's fourth outing is easily the weakest, for a few reasons. The most obvious is that gentle but still brave pivot away from mockumentary filmmaking. The presence of cameras and crew gives its cast a safety blanket so they still have plenty of room to do their wonderful interview schtick, but the overall narrative feels structurally flimsy as a result and there are a lot of little dips and pauses where the previous films flowed like hearty rivers.
