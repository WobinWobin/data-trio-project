Review by Ben 7

JAPAN CUTS 2024

I do not know where to begin with Kubi.

The cast is SUPER stacked. Like, I pretty much adore every single person involved with the film.

The violence is bonkers in a satisfyingly hilarious way. I cannot even begin to estimate the amount of beheadings and limbs lost in this masterwork.

Kitano leans as heavily as possible into dark comedy and straight-up slapstick, and every little wink at the camera and joke worked for me. You can tell the cast was having a blast throughout the film, and their energy is undoubtedly infectious and translates to the viewer.

The movie looks damn good. I loved the sets, battles, and everything in between.
