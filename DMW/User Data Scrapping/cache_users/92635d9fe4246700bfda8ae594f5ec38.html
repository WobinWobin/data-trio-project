Review by Justina Yam 3

some thoughts: i liked how although the protagonist is initially constantly interrogating white imperialism / the colonial nature of tourism, as well as the dichotomy of first/third world binaries, and yet she still ends up marrying a white love interest. the film has the capacity to engage with this kind of discourse, but her choices r still between two ignorant white dudes— one of which is fetishistic and orientalist, the other who thinks india is less progressive and backwards. this is just one example of how the film constantly flunctuates between being a feminist, genre-bending retelling and  reproduction of imperialistic hereropatriarchal narratives. Still pretty good for its time and def enjoyed the genre blending itself tho! No one else was tryin to do transnational discourse like this in mainstream romcom / Bollywood in 2004
