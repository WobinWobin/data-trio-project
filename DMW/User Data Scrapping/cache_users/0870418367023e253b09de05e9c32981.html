Howard

2018

Directed by Don Hahn

Synopsis

He gave a mermaid her voice and a beast his soul.

Though legendary lyricist Howard Ashman died far too young, his impact on Broadway, movies, and the culture at large were incalculable. Told entirely through rare archival footage and interviews with Ashman’s family, friends, associates, and longtime partner Bill Lauch, Howard is an intimate tribute to a once-in-a-generation talent and a rousing celebration of musical storytelling itself.

Review by KJL5678

Howard Ashman was a true luminary.

The fact that he accomplished as much as he did in his 40 years on this earth is laudable. I grew up loving "Little Shop of Horrors". It’s campy, sweet, moving, and brilliantly ridiculous.

I’m glad that he’s been credited as being one of the driving forces of Disney’s Renaissance as well. If not for his genius, we would not have had any of these gems up to the standard that he helped to create in "The Little Mermaid", "Beauty and the Beast" and "Aladdin", among others.

What a horrific loss. But what an incredible legacy he left for generations to come, and enjoy his unique magic. Plus, the artists he will continue to inspire along the way!
