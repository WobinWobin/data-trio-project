Synopsis

Review by Rafael "Mister Movie" Jovine 3

Harrowing.

A documentary that doesn’t shy away from showing the most horrific things happening during the Russian attacks on Mariupol. From the desperation of its citizens to the deaths and the somewhat graphic depiction of a newborn child, its depiction and portrayal of these events are raw and real. Chernov and the group of AP journalists do a great job placing us at the center of the action. It never crosses into sensationalism or Mondo, which is greatly appreciated, but as someone who has just heard of the event and has seen a few pieces of footage, this documentary was really eye-opening.

All in all, it definitely doesn’t make for an easy watch and discretion is advised, but if you are curious about seeing some first-hand accounts of this event, its definitely a must-watch.

Also, is it me, or does Chernov sound like Mark Cousins?
