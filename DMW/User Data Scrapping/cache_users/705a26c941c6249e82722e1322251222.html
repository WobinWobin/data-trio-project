Robot Jox

1989

Directed by Stuart Gordon

Synopsis

The ultimate killing machine. Part Man. Part Metal.

50 years after a nuclear war, the two superpowers handle territorial disputes in a different way. Each fields a giant robot to fight one-on-one battles in official matches, each piloted by a man inside, known as robot jockeys or jox. The contest for possession of Alaska will be fought by two of the best. The conscientious Achilles fights for the Americans. Opposing him is a Russian, Alexander.

Review by Matt! 3

“We can both live!”
“We are dead. We are Robot Jox.”

Stuart Gordon, are you kidding me?? There’s no way people can dislike this movie. Pacific Rim meets Cold War gladiatorial dystopia meets Starship Troopers-esque satire meets a Rocky IV winner-take-all fight where the grand prize is ownership of Alaska. The concept is legit ambitious and cool as hell, with practical effects that low key rock and an execution that comes off as both campy and dark at the same time—feels like something that should’ve come out earlier than 1989. Righteous. Also, only in a Charles Band production can you find the utmost self-seriousness and cynical USA v Russia metaphors while also featuring an official court panel of judges dressed as referees and a climactic scene where a giant robot teabags another giant robot with his giant chainsaw dick.
