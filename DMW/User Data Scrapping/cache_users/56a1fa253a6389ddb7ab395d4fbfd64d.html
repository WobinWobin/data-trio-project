Miroirs No. 3

2025
Watched 25 Mar 2026

My first Petzold was Transit at the Panamá Film Festival seven years ago. Latest Petzold was Miroirs No. 3 at D’A Film Festival in Barcelona with his presence. I watched Afire on my laptop while studying abroad in Germany. I’ve been big and small and big and small and big and small again all throughout Petzold’s filmography.

I wonder where the next one will take me, both physically and emotionally, as once again he’s left me feeling disoriented but glad to be where I am.
