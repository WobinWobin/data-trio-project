The Battle of Chile: Part III

1979
Watched 03 May 2026

Part I review
Part II review

Fascinating to see that Part III was not, as I anticipated, an examination of what Pinochet did when he stole power.

Instead Guzmán focuses on the workers and various groups who tried to work together to keep socialism strong in Chile, attempting to overcome some divisions and disagreemengs to keep Allende's politics alive. So interested in why Guzmán chose this path and in how correct he is to do so.

History has chronicled Pinochet's crimes in detail - it has not done the same with the solidarity of the Chilean working classes in supporting each other under a fascist power-grab.
