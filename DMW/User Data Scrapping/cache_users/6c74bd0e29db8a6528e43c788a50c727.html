Review by James (Schaffrillas)

Over the past 2 months I've had the pleasure of reading the excellent Jim Henson biography by Brian Jay Jones, which does an absolutely tremendous job of painting a portrait of who this guy is both creatively and personally. I wasn't expecting this documentary to include nearly the same level of detail, but I was nonetheless rather disappointed at how little I got a sense of Jim as a person from this. After a while it mostly becomes an engaging enough doc about his most well-known projects (though The Storyteller and Jim Henson Hour really get shafted - would have liked a lot more elaboration on those!) Ultimately this was pretty solid but I would genuinely recommend the Defunctland miniseries…

Review by Megan (stoobs) Cruz

As expected, I cried. This is a very enjoyable, if all too brief, glimpse into the magical mind & life of Jim Henson. His ambition, dedication & spirit lovingly described by family & collaborators alongside some incredible footage. There’s a behind the scenes look at the early days of Sesame Street & his friendship with Frank Oz as they developed characters like Bert & Ernie but they also spend a good amount of time delving into his early experimental films & wild fascinations. It starts to feel rushed in the last half hour & in general I wish it had been longer. There’s more than enough material for a multi season docuseries but I will take what I can get. Jim Henson, we love you ♥️
