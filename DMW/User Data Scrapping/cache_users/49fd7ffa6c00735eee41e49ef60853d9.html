Synopsis

When he said I do, he never said what he did.

A fearless, globe-trotting, terrorist-battling secret agent has his life turned upside down when he discovers his wife might be having an affair with a used car salesman while terrorists smuggle nuclear war heads into the United States.

Review by Todd Gaines 20

I’d forgotten how humorous True Lies is. It’s a comedic parody about a James Bond-esque secret agent, and the double life he lives with his unsuspecting wife. With the laughs, comes a lot of action and crazy stunts. Arnie, Jamie Lee Curtis, Bill fuckin’ Paxton, and yes, even Tom Arnold give unforgettable performances.

Terrorists are inane, asinine, heedless and daft. They deserve to be ridiculed, derided, lampooned and jeered at. James Cameron holds nothing back in depicting the radical extreme terrorists as complete and total buffoons. Nothing they say or do, makes any sense. You laugh like hell when the terrorist leader is making a propaganda video, and his camcorder’s battery runs dead. These bad guys ain’t the sharpest tools…
